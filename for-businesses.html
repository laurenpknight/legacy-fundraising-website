For Businesses

Support local programs. Connect with local customers.

Legacy Gold Card partners provide a valuable discount or complimentary offer that encourages families to visit their business while supporting student programs.

Why participate

Reach local families

Your offer is shared through participants, parents, supporters and school-connected networks.

Build community goodwill

Demonstrate visible support for programs that matter to your customers and employees.

Encourage repeat visits

A useful, well-designed offer can introduce new customers and create return traffic.

Participate without writing a large check

Support the community by providing an offer that brings customers through your doors.

A community win

The program receives funding. The supporter receives savings. The business gains exposure and customer traffic. The community grows stronger.
