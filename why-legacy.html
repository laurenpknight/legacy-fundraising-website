Why Legacy

A fundraising partner—not another platform to manage.

Technology can help a campaign reach more people. But technology alone does not create participation, confidence, accountability or community connection. Legacy combines modern tools with tangible supporter value and hands-on campaign partnership.

More than a product. More than a platform.

Tangible value for supporters

Gold Cards feature local discounts, complimentary offers and savings supporters can use long after the campaign ends.

A proven playbook for participants

Participants receive messaging, sales tactics, sharing tools, goals and practical training—not simply a stack of cards or a fundraising link.

A real partner for leaders

Legacy provides the strategy, technology and active campaign support so coaches and volunteers do not have to invent or administer the fundraiser themselves.

A stronger community connection

Participants engage the people around them. Local businesses gain visibility and customers. Supporters receive value. Programs fund opportunities for students.
