About

Building stronger communities, one program at a time.

Strong programs create opportunities for students. They provide belonging, leadership, discipline, creativity, competition and experiences that can shape a lifetime. But those opportunities require funding.

Legacy Fundraising exists to connect programs with the families, supporters and local businesses that want to invest in them. We combine tangible supporter value, participant-powered outreach, modern technology and hands-on campaign guidance to help programs fund their goals in a focused 14-day campaign.

We do more than provide a product. We provide the playbook.

Our purpose

To help student programs generate meaningful funding while strengthening the relationships between students, schools, supporters and local businesses.

Our promise

A focused campaign. A clear plan. Real supporter value. Active partnership. Community impact.

The community flywheel

Fundraising that fuels more than a budget.

Local businesses provide value

Participating merchants connect with families, gain visibility and support programs in their community.

Participants build connection

Participants learn to communicate, present value, set goals and represent their program.

Supporters save

Families receive discounts and offers they can use throughout the year.

Programs create opportunity

Funds support equipment, travel, uniforms, camps, competitions, facilities and student experiences.

When programs thrive, communities thrive.
