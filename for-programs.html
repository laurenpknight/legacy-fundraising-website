For Programs

You build the program. We power the campaign.

Legacy works with sports teams, fine arts programs, academic clubs and community organizations that need meaningful funding without adding another major administrative responsibility.

For coaches

You lead practices, manage schedules, develop participants and prepare for competition or performance. Legacy provides the fundraising system. Your role is to support the kickoff, reinforce the goal and encourage participation. We provide the tools, training, messages and campaign structure.

→
For program directors

Legacy can help create a consistent fundraising experience across programs: a defined 14-day campaign model, clear program-profit expectations, participant-focused execution, less administrative pressure on coaches, tangible and digital supporter options, a direct fundraising partner and community business engagement.

→
For booster clubs

Your volunteers should support the program—not spend weeks inventing a fundraiser. Legacy provides campaign planning, communication templates, participant training, sales tools and tactics, Gold Card support, digital fundraising options, active campaign guidance, and a clear beginning and end.
