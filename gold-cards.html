Gold Cards

Support the program. Save all year.

Legacy Gold Cards give supporters valuable local discounts, complimentary offers and savings while helping fund student programs in their community.

Real local value

Offers are designed around businesses and services families already use.

Tangible and easy to understand

Supporters receive a physical Gold Card they can keep and use throughout the campaign period.

More than a donation

A supporter can help a program while receiving something valuable in return.

Digital support is still available

Friends, family and supporters outside the local area can participate through digital giving options.

Why supporters say yes

  • They want to support local students.
  • They recognize the participating businesses.
  • They can use the savings throughout the year.
  • The card can provide value beyond its purchase price.
  • The purchase strengthens the local community.
